How this is checked, and what it does not promise

  • Days are counted in UTC. Two tweets in one UTC day count once, so spacing them out is the point.
  • The posting time comes from the tweet itself, not from when you paste it. Late submissions of an earlier tweet are fine; backdating is not possible.
  • Deleting a tweet undoes it. Every tweet is re-checked before rewards, and a deleted one stops counting.
  • One X account per wallet, one wallet per X account. The first tweet you verify binds the two together.
  • Private accounts cannot be verified, because we can only read public posts.
  • This is a list, not a payment. Completing the tasks records your address for future rewards. It is not a purchase, it carries no guarantee of value, and nothing is owed to you by taking part.
