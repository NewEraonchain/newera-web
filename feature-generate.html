Each step, in detail

The plain explanation on top, what actually runs underneath.

01

Write your prompt

Type what you want to see: a subject, a style, a mood. Then press Generate.

runs

Nothing is created yet. The app sends your prompt to NewEra along with your login, so the charge and the image are tied to your account.

02

10 NEA comes off your balance

There's nothing to approve and no wallet pop-up. The cost is taken from your NEA balance as the request goes in.

runs

The charge is a single guarded update: it only goes through if your balance covers it, so a balance can never go negative and the same NEA can't be spent twice. A history entry is written with it.

03

No image, no charge

If anything goes wrong before your image exists, the 10 NEA goes straight back to your balance.

runs

From the moment you're charged until the image is saved, any failure triggers an automatic refund, recorded in your history as its own entry. You're never charged for an image you didn't get.

04

The AI renders your image

With the charge made, your prompt becomes a new image, usually within seconds.

runs

The prompt is sent to the image model. The result is stored with your prompt, under your account.

05

You own it

The image lands in your gallery. Keep it, download it, or list it for sale.

runs

It's recorded with your account as owner, the one you sign in to with your wallet. If you sell it later, ownership moves to the buyer in the same step as the payment.

The words you'll see

New to this? Here's the plain meaning of each term.

NEA
NewEra's platform credit. You spend it to generate (10 per image) and earn it by selling your work. It lives in your NewEra account, has no monetary value, and can't be withdrawn.
Wallet
Your login. Signing a free message proves who you are, with no email or password. It never sends a transaction, and you never need gas.
Account history
A record of every change to your NEA balance: what it was for, how much, and the balance after. Nothing moves without an entry.
On-chain (later)
NewEra's first phase ran on BNB Chain. On-chain settlement is paused so creating is instant and free of gas, and it returns in a later phase.

Why it works this way

You own itThe image is recorded to your account the moment it's made, and it stays yours until you choose to sell it.
It's fairYou're charged only for images you actually receive. Failed generations are refunded automatically.
No middlemanWhen you sell, the earnings settle straight to your balance.
