How a sale works

From listing your image to NEA in your balance.

01

List your image

Pick an image you own, set a price in NEA, and list it. Listing is free.

runs

Your listing is saved and shown to buyers right away. You stay the owner until someone buys it.

02

A buyer purchases it

A buyer clicks Buy. The price is taken from their NEA balance, with nothing to approve.

runs

The purchase is a single all-or-nothing step: the listing is claimed, the buyer is charged the listing's own price, you are credited, and ownership moves. If any part fails, none of it happens.

03

The payment splits automatically

The price is divided the instant the sale goes through: 95% to you, 5% platform fee.

runs

The split is the same for every sale and is applied in the same step that completes it. If two people try to buy the same image at once, exactly one succeeds and the other is not charged.

04

You're paid, the buyer unlocks

Your 95% lands in your balance immediately. The buyer gets the full image to download.

runs

NewEra records the sale, moves the image into the buyer's collection, and unlocks the download. An image sells once, then leaves the market.

What the split means for you

Two numbers, plainly.

95% creator
The largest share is yours. On a 100 NEA sale, 95 NEA goes to your balance, in the same step as the sale.
5% platform fee
A small share that funds running and improving NewEra. It's the same for every sale.
