How a sale works

From listing your image to money in your wallet.

01

List your image

Pick an image you own, set a price in NEA, and list it. Listing is free.

runs

Your listing is registered with the marketplace contract and shown to buyers. You stay the owner until someone buys it, and you can remove the listing any time.

02

A buyer purchases it

A buyer approves the price from their wallet and confirms the purchase.

runs

The buyer's wallet runs an approve for the price, then buy() on the contract. This single call moves the NEA and triggers the split, all in one transaction.

03

The payment splits automatically

The contract divides the price the instant the sale goes through: 95% to you, 5% platform fee.

runs

The split is written into the contract itself, so no one can change the percentages or skim the middle. Each share is transferred in the same transaction that completes the sale.

04

You're paid, the buyer unlocks

Your 95% lands in your wallet immediately. The buyer gets the full image to download.

runs

NewEra reads the purchase transaction from the chain to confirm it really happened, records the sale, and unlocks the download for the buyer. The same purchase can't be reused.

What the split means for you

Three numbers, plainly.

95% creator
The largest share is yours. On a 100 NEA sale, 80 NEA goes to your wallet, settled in the same transaction as the sale.
A platform share that funds running and improving NewEra. It's fixed in the contract, the same for every sale.
5% platform fee
Sent to a burn address nobody controls, permanently removing it from circulation. As more sells happen, the total NEA supply slowly shrinks.
