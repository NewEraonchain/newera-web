FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a crypto wallet?
Yes. You need MetaMask (or any compatible Web3 wallet) to connect. Your wallet is your login, with no email or password required. Signing in is a free signature: no funds move and you never pay gas.
What is NEA and how do I get it?
NEA is NewEra's platform credit. It lives in your NewEra account, has no monetary value, and can't be withdrawn or exchanged. New users get a 50 NEA welcome bonus on their first connect, and you earn more by selling your work and through referrals. You use NEA to generate images and trade on the marketplace.
How much does it cost to generate an image?
Each image costs 10 NEA, charged from your balance when you generate. If a generation fails, you're refunded automatically.
How do earnings work when I sell?
Every sale is split automatically: 95% goes to you (the creator), and 5% is a platform fee. Your share is credited to your balance the moment the sale completes.
Do I really own my images?
Yes. Every image you generate is recorded to your account and appears in your collection. When you sell one, ownership moves to the buyer and it leaves your collection. On-chain proof of ownership is paused for now and returns in a later phase.
Is this real money right now?
No. NEA is a free platform credit with no monetary value, and it can't be withdrawn or exchanged. NewEra ran on BNB Chain during its first phase. On-chain settlement is paused for now and returns in a later phase.
