How a prompt sale works

Listing keeps your text private until it's paid for.

01

List a prompt with its result image

Add the image this prompt creates, give it a title, and set a price in NEA. The image is public; the prompt text stays hidden.

runs

The marketplace shows the result image, title, and a short preview to everyone, so buyers can judge the quality. The actual prompt text is held back until a purchase is confirmed.

02

A buyer pays for it

A buyer sees the title, decides it's worth it, and confirms the price from their wallet.

runs

The buyer's wallet runs an approve then buy() on the contract, moving the NEA in a single transaction and producing a receipt.

03

The payment splits on-chain

The price is divided automatically: 95% to you, 5% platform fee.

runs

The same 80/15/5 split as the image marketplace, written into the contract. Each share transfers in the same transaction that completes the sale.

04

The text unlocks for the buyer

Once payment is verified, the full prompt is revealed to the buyer to copy and use.

runs

NewEra confirms the purchase transaction on-chain, records the sale, and reveals the full prompt text to that buyer only. Your 95% is already in your wallet.

Why prompts are worth selling

The wording is the skill. Here's why it has value.

The prompt is the craft
Getting a model to produce a specific look takes experiment and taste. A proven prompt saves the buyer that work, so it has real value on its own.
See the result, not the recipe
Buyers can see the image the prompt produces, so they know the quality they're getting. The prompt text itself only reveals after purchase, so you sell it without giving it away.
Sell it again and again
A prompt isn't used up when it sells. The same listing can be bought by many people, and you earn your share on every sale.

What you should know

You keep 95%The same creator share as images, settled to your wallet the moment a prompt sells.
Preview is publicTitle and a short teaser are visible to all; the full text is not, until someone buys.
Buyers get usageA buyer can read and use the prompt themselves. They don't get the right to resell your listing.
