Every figure above comes from these public endpoints on this site. Open any of them in a browser and you will see the same raw numbers this page is built from.

  • /api/analytics/overview — every headline number on this page

Being straight about what this does and does not prove: these endpoints report what the NewEra platform recorded. They are our records, published openly, not an independent audit.
