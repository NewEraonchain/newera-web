How each one pays

Where the money comes from, and when it lands.

01

Selling images & prompts

List your work at any price. When it sells, you keep 95%. The rest is a 5% platform fee.

runs

The 95/5 split is written into the marketplace contract. Your 95% transfers to your wallet in the very same transaction that completes the sale, so there's nothing to claim.

02

Referral rewards

Share your referral link. When someone you referred generates their first image, you get 10 NEA.

runs

The reward is sent from the treasury to your wallet on-chain once the referred user's first generation is confirmed. It pays once per referred creator.

03

Welcome bonus

New wallets receive 50 NEA on their first connect, enough to start generating right away.

runs

This is a one-time claim per wallet, drawn from a fixed reserve set aside in the token supply. It can't be claimed twice.

What "no middleman" actually means

The difference from a normal platform, in plain terms.

No payout button
On most platforms you earn a balance, then request a withdrawal and wait. Here your share arrives in your wallet the moment a sale clears. There's nothing to withdraw.
No one holds your money
The platform never takes custody of your earnings. The contract moves NEA directly from buyer to you, so it can't be frozen or delayed by anyone.
The rules are fixed
The 95% share and the reward amounts are set in the contract, the same for everyone. No surprise fee changes, no tiers, no fine print.

Good to know

Paid in NEAAll earnings are in NEA, the platform token. You can spend it on generating, or hold it in your wallet.
Track it on-chainEvery sale and reward is a real transaction. You can look up exactly what you earned and when.
Gas still appliesTransactions need a small BNB gas fee, separate from your NEA earnings. You'll need a small amount of BNB for gas.
