What "no middleman" actually means

The difference from a normal platform, in plain terms.

No payout button
On most platforms you earn a balance, then request a withdrawal and wait. Here your share arrives in your balance the moment a sale clears, ready to spend.
Plain about what NEA is
NEA is a platform credit held in your NewEra account. It has no monetary value and can't be withdrawn or exchanged, so there is no money for anyone to hold or delay.
The rules are fixed
The 95% share and the reward amounts are the same for everyone. No surprise fee changes, no tiers, no fine print.
